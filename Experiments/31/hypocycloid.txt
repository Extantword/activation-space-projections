HYPOCYCLOID (CUSPED CURVES)
===========================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of hypocycloid curves — the path
traced by a point on the rim of a circle rolling inside a larger fixed
circle.  When the ratio R/r = k is an integer, the curve has exactly k
outward-pointing cusps:  k=3 gives the deltoid, k=4 the astroid, etc.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Choose  k  from  {3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}.  Set R = 1, r = 1/k.
  2. Choose a random rotation angle.
  3. Evaluate at 801 points:
         x(t) = (R-r)*cos(t) + r*cos((R-r)*t/r)
         y(t) = (R-r)*sin(t) - r*sin((R-r)*t/r)
  4. Apply global rotation, scale to fit, draw as a polyline.


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry group:  D_k  (k rotations + k reflections)

The hypocycloid with integer ratio k has k cusps at equal angular
intervals.  It is a special case of the hypotrochoid (Experiment 6)
with pen distance d = r (the point is on the rolling circle's rim).

Notable special cases:
  - k=2 : line segment (degenerate)
  - k=3 : deltoid  — the envelope of the Simson lines of a triangle
  - k=4 : astroid  — has the equation  x^{2/3} + y^{2/3} = R^{2/3}

The arc length of a full k-cusped hypocycloid is  L = 8R(k-1)/k.
The enclosed area is  A = pi*R^2*(k-1)*(k-2)/k^2.

Parameter space:  (k, rotation) — 6 discrete cusp counts times a
continuous rotation angle.
