EPICYCLOID CURVES
=================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of epicycloid curves — the paths
traced by a point on a small circle rolling on the OUTSIDE of a fixed
circle.  These are the "outward" counterparts of the hypotrochoids
(Experiment 6, rolling inside).  Epicycloids produce cusped, lobed
patterns with rotational symmetry.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Randomise the curve parameters:
       - R  (fixed circle radius)   in  [8, 20]
       - r  (rolling circle radius) in  [3, 0.7*R]
       - d  (pen offset from rolling centre) in  [0.3*r, 1.2*r]

  2. Trace the curve over 20 revolutions (2000 sample points):

       x(t) = (R + r) * cos(t)  -  d * cos((R + r) * t / r)
       y(t) = (R + r) * sin(t)  -  d * sin((R + r) * t / r)

  3. Scale the point set to fit within the image (3-pixel margin).

  4. Draw the polyline (white on black).


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
An epitrochoid is traced by a point at distance d from the centre of a
circle of radius r rolling without slipping on the outside of a fixed
circle of radius R.

Special cases:
  - d = r :  epicycloid (the point is on the rim)
  - R = r :  the curve is a cardioid  (a single-cusped heart shape)
  - R = 2r:  the curve is a nephroid  (kidney shape, 2 cusps)
  - R/r = n (integer):  the curve has n cusps and C_n symmetry

The parametric equations are:
    x(t) = (R + r) cos(t) - d cos((R + r) t / r)
    y(t) = (R + r) sin(t) - d sin((R + r) t / r)

In complex notation:
    z(t) = (R + r) exp(it)  -  d exp(i(R+r)t/r)

This is a sum of two circular motions with frequencies +1 and +(R+r)/r.
The interference creates the lobed shape.

Comparison with hypotrochoid (Experiment 6):
  - Hypotrochoid: rolling circle INSIDE, frequencies +1 and -(R-r)/r
  - Epitrochoid:  rolling circle OUTSIDE, frequencies +1 and +(R+r)/r
  - Hypotrochoids tend to produce "inward" lobes; epicycloids produce
    "outward" cusps.

The maximum radial extent is  R + r + d,  and the minimum is
|R + r - d|.  When d > R + r, the curve has inner loops.

Rotational symmetry:  If R/r = p/q in lowest terms, the curve closes
after q turns and has p-fold symmetry (C_p).

Parameter space:  (R, r, d) — 3 continuous dimensions, effectively
2-D after rescaling.
