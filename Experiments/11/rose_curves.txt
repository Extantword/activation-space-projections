ROSE CURVES
===========

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of rhodonea (rose) curves — polar
curves of the form  r = cos(k*theta).  Depending on whether k is odd or
even, the curve produces k or 2k petals respectively, each arranged with
rotational symmetry around the origin.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Choose a petal number  k  from {2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}.
  2. Choose a random phase offset  phi  in  [0, 2*pi).
  3. Set  amplitude = size/2 - 3.
  4. Evaluate the parametric form at 601 values of  theta  in  [0, 2*pi]:

         r(theta) = amplitude * cos(k * theta + phi)
         x(theta) = cx + r * cos(theta)
         y(theta) = cy + r * sin(theta)

     When r < 0 the point naturally maps to the opposite side of the
     origin (theta + pi), which is how the second set of petals appears
     for even k.

  5. Draw the resulting polyline (white on black).


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry group:
  - k odd:   D_k  (k petals, k-fold rotation + k mirror axes)
  - k even:  D_{2k}  (2k petals, 2k-fold rotation + 2k mirror axes)

The rose curve  r = cos(k*theta)  is a special case of the more general
family  r = cos(k*theta + phi).  The phase phi rotates the entire figure
by  phi/k  radians.

For integer k the curve is algebraic (expressible as a polynomial in
x and y).  The Cartesian equation can be derived by substituting
r^2 = x^2 + y^2 and r*cos(theta) = x:

  - k=2:  (x^2 + y^2)^3 = 4x^2*y^2   (four-petalled rose)
  - k=3:  the equation is degree 8

The area enclosed by a single petal is  A = pi/(4k)  (in the unit
circle).  The total area of all petals is  A_total = pi/4  for odd k,
and  A_total = pi/2  for even k — independent of k.

Rose curves are related to Chebyshev polynomials: the radial function
cos(k*theta) = T_k(cos(theta)) where T_k is the k-th Chebyshev
polynomial of the first kind.

The phase parameter phi gives each petal number its own 1-D manifold
of orientations.  Combined with the discrete choice of k, the full
parameter space is  {2..8} x [0, 2*pi).
