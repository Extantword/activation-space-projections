BEATING RADIAL WAVES (AMPLITUDE-MODULATED RIPPLES)
===================================================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of concentric ripple patterns
with visible amplitude modulation (beats).  Two circular waves with
slightly different frequencies emanate from the image centre; their
sum creates the classic beating phenomenon where the ripple intensity
slowly pulses between loud and quiet zones.  Every pixel has a smooth,
continuously-computed intensity value, producing the rich wave-field
aesthetic of physical water ripples or acoustic interference.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Compute radial distance from the centre:
         r(x,y) = sqrt((x - cx)^2 + (y - cy)^2)

  2. Choose two close frequencies:
         f_base  in  [0.4, 1.2]
         df      in  [0.03, 0.15]
         f1 = f_base + df/2,   f2 = f_base - df/2

  3. Choose a random phase offset.

  4. Compute:
         pattern = sin(f1 * r + phase) + sin(f2 * r)

  5. Apply a circular fade:
         pattern *= clamp(1 - r / (size/2), 0, 1)

  6. Normalise to [0, 255].


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry:  SO(2)  (full continuous rotational symmetry)

The sum of two sinusoids can be rewritten using the product-to-sum
identity:
    sin(f1*r) + sin(f2*r) = 2 * cos(df*r/2) * sin(fc*r/2)

where  df = f1 - f2  (beat frequency) and  fc = f1 + f2  (carrier).

The carrier  sin(fc*r/2)  produces fine concentric rings.
The envelope  cos(df*r/2)  produces slow amplitude modulation.
The visible "beat length" (distance between envelope nodes) is
    L_beat = 2*pi / df.

Physical analogues:
  - Two tuning forks of slightly different pitch
  - Two stones dropped in a pond close together
  - Acoustic room modes with close frequencies

The circular fade envelope prevents aliasing artefacts at the image
boundary and gives the pattern a natural "pond ripple" look.

Parameter space:  (f_base, df, phase) — a 3-D continuous space.  The
ratio df / f_base controls how many beats are visible; f_base controls
the fineness of the carrier ripples.
