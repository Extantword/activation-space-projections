SUPERFORMULA CURVES (GIELIS SHAPES)
===================================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of curves generated by the Gielis
superformula — a single polar equation that can produce circles,
polygons, stars, flowers, and many organic-looking shapes depending
on its six parameters.  The symmetry parameter m determines the
rotational order.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Randomise parameters:
       - m   from  {3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}   (symmetry order)
       - a   in  [0.8, 1.2]
       - b   in  [0.8, 1.2]
       - n1  in  [0.3, 6.0]
       - n2  in  [0.5, 6.0]
       - n3  in  [0.5, 6.0]
       - rotation  in  [0, 2*pi)

  2. Evaluate at 801 points:
         r(theta) = [ |cos(m*theta/4)/a|^n2
                      + |sin(m*theta/4)/b|^n3 ]^(-1/n1)

  3. Convert to Cartesian, apply rotation, scale, draw polyline.


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry group:  C_m  when a != b or n2 != n3;  D_m  when a = b
                  and n2 = n3.

The superformula was introduced by Johan Gielis (2003) as a
generalisation of the superellipse (Lame curve).  It unifies many
classical curves:

  - m=4, n1=n2=n3=2, a=b=1 :  circle
  - m=4, n1=2, n2=n3=large :  rounded square → square as n2,n3 → ∞
  - m=4, n1=0.5, n2=n3=0.5 :  4-pointed star (astroid-like)
  - m=3, n1=n2=n3=2 :  rounded triangle
  - m=0, any n :  circle (degenerate)

The parameter n1 controls overall convexity:
  - n1 > 1 :  convex (rounded)
  - n1 < 1 :  concave (star-like, with indentations)
  - n1 → 0 :  extreme star with narrow rays

Parameters n2 and n3 control the "sharpness" of the lobes.  When
n2 = n3, the curve has additional mirror symmetry within each lobe.
When n2 != n3, the lobes are asymmetric, breaking D_m down to C_m.

The curve is always a closed loop (r is periodic with period 2*pi
when m is even, period 4*pi when m is odd — but the plotted shape
repeats after 2*pi for integer m).

Parameter space:  (m, a, b, n1, n2, n3, rotation) — 6 discrete m
values times a 6-D continuous shape space.
