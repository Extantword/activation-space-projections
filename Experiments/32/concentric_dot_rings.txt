CONCENTRIC DOT RINGS
====================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images showing dots arranged on concentric
rings.  Each k-th ring holds  fold * k  equally-spaced dots, so the
density increases outward.  The full pattern has D_{fold} symmetry.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Choose  fold  from  {3, 4, 5, 6, 8}.
  2. Choose  num_rings  in  {3, ..., 7}, a dot radius, and a phase offset.
  3. For ring k = 1 .. num_rings:
         r        = max_r * k / num_rings
         num_dots = fold * k
         Place num_dots equally-spaced filled circles on the ring.


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry group:  D_{fold}

Each ring individually has D_{num_dots} symmetry (a regular polygon of
dots).  The intersection of all these dihedral groups is D_{fold},
since fold divides every num_dots = fold * k.

The total number of dots is  fold * sum_{k=1}^{num_rings} k
= fold * num_rings * (num_rings + 1) / 2,  ranging from 18 to 168.

The pattern resembles the dot layouts used in halftone printing and
in the rosette arrangements found in certain crystal diffraction
patterns.

Parameter space:  (fold, num_rings, dot_r, phase) — 5 discrete
symmetry classes times a 3-D continuous subspace.
