FRIEZE PATTERNS
===============

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of frieze (strip) patterns.  A small
random motif is drawn within one cell and then tiled horizontally across
the entire image width.  Each cell also has internal bilateral symmetry
(a vertical mirror within the cell), producing one of the classic frieze-
group symmetry types.  The result is a vertically-spanning, horizontally-
repeating ornamental strip.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Choose a cell width:  cell_w  from  {8, 16, 32}  pixels.
     Number of cells:  num_cells = size / cell_w.

  2. Generate a random motif in the right half of one cell:
       - num_strokes  in  {2, 3, 4, 5}
       - For each stroke, two random endpoints:
             x1, x2  in  [cell_w/2, cell_w-1]
             y1, y2  in  [2, size-2]

  3. For each cell  c = 0, 1, ..., num_cells-1:

       x_offset = c * cell_w

       For each stroke (x1, y1, x2, y2):
         a. Draw the right-half stroke at (x_offset + x1, y1) to
            (x_offset + x2, y2).
         b. Draw the mirrored left-half stroke at (x_offset + cell_w - x1, y1)
            to (x_offset + cell_w - x2, y2).


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry group:  a frieze group containing translations and reflections

The seven frieze groups (the only symmetry groups of a strip pattern)
are classified by their combination of:
  - Translation (T):         always present
  - Vertical reflection (V): mirror perpendicular to the strip
  - Horizontal reflection (H): mirror along the strip
  - Glide reflection (G):   translation + horizontal reflection
  - 180-degree rotation (R): half-turn

The seven groups:
  p1    : T only
  p11m  : T + H
  p11g  : T + G
  p2    : T + R
  p2mm  : T + V + H + R
  p2mg  : T + V + G + R
  p2gm  : T + G + H + R (effectively same as p2mg up to alignment)

Our construction enforces:
  - Translation with period cell_w
  - Vertical mirror within each cell
This corresponds to frieze group p2mm (if the motif also has horizontal
symmetry) or p11m (vertical mirrors plus translation).

Frieze patterns are among the most ancient decorative motifs, appearing
in Greek pottery, Maori carvings, and Art Deco architecture.

The image as a whole has approximate translational symmetry (exact up to
boundary effects at the image edges).  With num_cells = 4 to 8
repetitions visible, the periodicity is clearly perceptible.

Parameter space:  (cell_w, num_strokes, stroke endpoints) — a variable-
dimensional space.  For 4 strokes, each cell motif has 4 * 4 = 16 real
parameters (the right-half endpoints), and the tiling is deterministic.
