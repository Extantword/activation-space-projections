COMPLETE GRAPH ON REGULAR n-GON
===============================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of complete graphs K_n.  The n
vertices are placed on a regular polygon and every pair is connected
by a straight line, producing a dense web of chords with D_n dihedral
symmetry.  The resulting pattern shows intricate internal star and
polygon sub-structures.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Choose  n  in  {5, ..., 18}.
  2. Place vertices on a circle of radius  size/2 - 3  with a random
     phase offset.
  3. Draw all  C(n,2) = n*(n-1)/2  edges.
  4. Brightness per edge is scaled inversely with n to prevent
     saturation at high vertex counts.


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry group:  D_n  (n rotations + n reflections)

The complete graph K_n on vertices of a regular n-gon has:
  - n vertices
  - n(n-1)/2 edges
  - Each vertex has degree n-1

The pattern of chord intersections inside the circle is related to
the "mystic rose" or "string art" construction.  The number of
interior intersection points (for n vertices in general position on
a circle) is C(n,4) = n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)/24, since each 4-subset of
vertices defines a unique interior crossing of two chords.

The set of distinct chord lengths in K_n on a regular n-gon is
floor(n/2), corresponding to chords spanning 1, 2, ..., floor(n/2)
vertices.  Chords of the same span form a regular star polygon.

For prime n, the chord pattern has no non-trivial sub-symmetries,
making the full D_n group the only symmetry.  For composite n, there
are additional sub-patterns with higher symmetry (e.g. K_12 contains
a K_6 sub-pattern with D_6 symmetry).

Parameter space:  (n, phase) — 14 discrete vertex counts times a
continuous rotation angle.
