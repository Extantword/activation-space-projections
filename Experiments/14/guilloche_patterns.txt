GUILLOCHE PATTERNS
==================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of guilloche (security-engraving)
patterns.  Each image overlays two hypotrochoid curves drawn with
slightly different pen distances and a phase offset.  The interplay
between the two curves creates the fine interlocking line work seen on
banknotes, certificates, and passport pages.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Choose shared parameters for both curves:
       - R  (fixed circle radius)    in  [14, 25]
       - r  (rolling circle radius)  in  [3, 0.6*R]

  2. Choose per-curve pen distances:
       - d1  in  [0.4*r, 1.0*r]
       - d2  in  [0.4*r, 1.0*r]

  3. Choose a phase offset between the two curves:
       - phase_offset  in  [pi/12, pi/3]

  4. For each curve, evaluate the hypotrochoid over 20 revolutions
     (2000 sample points):

       Curve 1 (phase = 0):
         x(t) = (R-r)*cos(t) + d1*cos((R-r)*t/r)
         y(t) = (R-r)*sin(t) - d1*sin((R-r)*t/r)

       Curve 2 (phase = phase_offset):
         Same equations with d2 and  t -> t + phase_offset.

  5. Scale each curve independently to fit the image.

  6. Draw both curves (grey on black) on the same canvas.


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Each individual curve is a hypotrochoid (see Experiment 6).  The
guilloche pattern is the visual superposition of two such curves.

When the two curves share the same (R, r) but differ in d or phase,
they have the same rotational symmetry order  n = round(R/r)  but
different radial extents, creating a moire-like region between them
where the curves interleave.

The envelope (boundary) of the region between the two curves forms a
smooth closed curve whose shape depends on d1, d2, and the phase offset.

Historically, guilloche patterns were produced by geometric lathes —
mechanical devices that simultaneously rotate and translate a cutting
tool.  The difficulty of replicating these patterns by hand made them
an early anti-counterfeiting measure.

The mathematical description is a pair of parametric curves in the
plane, each periodic with period  T = 2*pi*r / gcd(R-r, r)  (when
R/r is rational).  The two curves together form a 2-component link in
3-D (parameter, x, y) space.

Parameter space:  (R, r, d1, d2, phase_offset) — 5 continuous
dimensions.  Since only the ratios matter (due to rescaling), the
effective dimensionality is 4.
