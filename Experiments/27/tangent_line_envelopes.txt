TANGENT LINE ENVELOPES
======================

1. WHAT IS IT
-------------
A dataset of 64x64 grayscale images of tangent-line patterns.  A family
of straight lines, each tangent to a hidden circle, is drawn across the
image.  The envelope of the line family (the curve all lines touch) is
the circle itself, but the collection of tangent lines fills the image
with a dense, luminous pattern of overlapping lines that has continuous
rotational symmetry.


2. GENERATION ALGORITHM
-----------------------
For each image:

  1. Place the centre at (cx, cy) = (size/2, size/2).

  2. Randomise:
       - circle_r   in  [8, 0.3*size]   (radius of the hidden circle)
       - num_lines  in  {20, ..., 60}

  3. For each line  j = 0, 1, ..., num_lines-1:

       a. Compute the tangent angle:
              theta_j = 2*pi * j / num_lines

       b. The point of tangency on the circle:
              T = (cx + circle_r * cos(theta_j),
                   cy + circle_r * sin(theta_j))

       c. The tangent direction (perpendicular to the radius):
              d = (-sin(theta_j),  cos(theta_j))

       d. Extend the line by  L = 0.8 * size  in both directions:
              P1 = T + L * d
              P2 = T - L * d

       e. Draw the line segment P1-P2 (grey on black).


3. MATHEMATICAL BACKGROUND
---------------------------
Symmetry group:  approximately C_{num_lines}  (discrete rotation),
                  exact SO(2) in the continuous limit.

The envelope of a one-parameter family of lines
    F(x, y, theta) = 0
is the curve satisfying both  F = 0  and  dF/d(theta) = 0.

For tangent lines to a circle of radius R centred at the origin:
    F(x, y, theta) = x*cos(theta) + y*sin(theta) - R

The envelope equations give:
    x = R*cos(theta),   y = R*sin(theta)   — the circle itself.

Each tangent line is at distance R from the centre.  The density of
line intersections is highest near the circle and decreases outward,
creating a bright annular "glow" around the envelope.

The number of intersection points of the full line family is
C(num_lines, 2) = num_lines * (num_lines - 1) / 2.  These intersections
form a dense point set that approximates the circle for large num_lines.

Dual interpretation:  In projective duality, points and lines are
interchanged.  The circle (a conic) dualises to another conic, and the
tangent-line pattern is the dual representation of the circle.

Generalisations:
  - Tangent lines to an ellipse produce a similar pattern with D2
    symmetry.
  - Tangent lines to more complex curves (cardioid, astroid) produce
    richer envelopes with cusps and branches.

Parameter space:  (circle_r, num_lines) — a 2-D space (one continuous,
one discrete).  Visually, circle_r controls the size of the bright
ring, and num_lines controls the fineness of the line texture.
